# Generated Outputs

This document describes how software licenses relate to files produced with OOOSplat, including `final.ply`, exported Gaussian Splatting models, and intermediate project outputs.

## No automatic software-license inheritance

A generated output does not become subject to the OOOSplat Apache License 2.0, the GNU General Public License (GPL), the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL), or another bundled third-party software license merely because OOOSplat or one of its bundled tools was used to create or process it.

This statement only addresses automatic transmission of software-license terms through ordinary program use. It does not assign copyright ownership, grant rights in an output, or establish a default license for generated models.

## Rights and restrictions that may still apply

The rights available for a generated output depend on the facts of the particular project. They may be affected by:

- rights and license terms governing the input video, images, audio, metadata, or other source material;
- protected material, program code, model data, or other content copied or embedded in the output;
- third-party service or dataset terms;
- employment, client, contributor, or other contractual obligations; and
- applicable copyright, privacy, publicity, trademark, export-control, or other law.

The general rule above does not apply to the extent an output actually contains or derives from protected program code or material in a manner that independently triggers a license or other legal obligation.

You are responsible for confirming that you have the rights needed for your inputs and intended use of the outputs. OOOSplat makes no determination about ownership of a generated model and grants no rights in third-party material.
